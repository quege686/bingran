SECTION 04

版权安全底座

所有原创迷彩均须遵循版权安全流程，确保图案原创性可追溯、可验证

🔄 原创迷彩开发全流程

从 AI 生成到上架，每一步都需留存记录，确保版权链完整：

AI 生成 → 人工二次修改 → 保存原始生成记录 → 保存 PSD/AI 源文件 → 记录设计日期 → IP 筛查 → 上架

📋 万能版权安全 Prompt 底座

以后团队开发任何原创迷彩，都可以把这一段放到 Prompt 最后：

IMPORTANT ORIGINALITY REQUIREMENTS: Create the camouflage pattern entirely from scratch. Do not trace, reproduce, recreate, modify, redraw, imitate or closely resemble any existing military camouflage, commercial camouflage pattern, fashion brand pattern or copyrighted textile design. Do not reference MultiCam, MARPAT, OCP, A-TACS, CADPAT, Flecktarn, DPM or other recognizable camouflage patterns. Use an independently developed visual language, original shapes, original proportions, original color relationships and original repeat structure. The final pattern must be visually distinct from existing camouflage designs and intended as an original commercial fashion textile artwork. No logos, trademarks, military insignia, national emblems or recognizable symbols.

⚠️ 重要现实问题

AI Prompt 写了 "original" ≠ 法律上自动获得"绝对无版权风险"。真正做 TEMU 商品时，建议不要让商家直接拿 AI 出图就上架，必须经过完整的人工修改与 IP 筛查流程。
